    Can my customer sign digitally if I'm not at the job site in person?
    Yes. Simply toggle off the On-Spot Customer E-Signature Pad to generate a clean contract with both a physical signature line and interactive digital signature fields. You can send the contract as a Shareable Link (where your client signs right on their phone screen), an Interactive PDF (with fillable digital signature boxes), or an editable MS Word document.
    What happens if I leave certain fields blank?
    Any field left blank—such as a license number, secondary address, completion date, or pre-existing damage note—is automatically omitted from both the live preview and the final exported contract so your paperwork always looks clean and custom-drafted.
