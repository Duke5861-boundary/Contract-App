Are these documents legally binding?

BuildWaivers provides self-service software and template generator software; we are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice. While our templates include standard business protections (like late payment fees and IP retention clauses), no software can guarantee court enforceability. If you require legal advice or guaranteed enforceability for your specific state, you must consult a licensed attorney.
